# Third-Party Notices

## Wave.js

The radial drawing concepts used by `CrystalAudioVisualizer` are adapted from
the `Glob`, `Shine`, and `Circles` animations in Wave.js:

- Source: https://github.com/foobar404/wave.js
- License: MIT

The MelodyVision adaptation adds lifecycle cleanup, frequency-aware band
mapping, reduced-motion behavior, and a crystal-specific rendering system.
